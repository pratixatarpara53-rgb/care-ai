Understanding Risk Levels

🟢 Low Minor symptoms, no immediate threat. Monitor at home; contact ASHA worker if worsening.
🟡 Moderate Needs attention within 24–48 hours. Visit your nearest PHC or healthcare worker.
🔴 Urgent Requires immediate care. Call 108 or go to hospital emergency NOW.

Doctor Consultation

GP or specialist for complex conditions, prescription management

🏨
District / Sub-district Hospital

Moderate to serious conditions, labs, specialists, overnight care
